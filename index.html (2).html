    🍶 Curated Yokocho & Special Food Guide

    NIGHT 1 • ASAKUSA

    Asakusa Hoppy Street

    ホッピー通り

    Covered outdoor street counters serving slow-simmered beef tendon stew (Suji-Nikomi), Monjayaki, and grilled skewers. Very welcoming to family groups early evening (17:30 – 19:30 PM).

    NIGHT 2 • SHINJUKU

    Shinjuku Omoide Yokocho

    思ひ出横丁 (Memory Lane)

    Iconic red paper lantern alleyways under train tracks serving charcoal Yakitori skewers, ramen, and fried Gyoza counters. Atmospheric post-Fuji celebration.

    NIGHT 3 • UENO

    Ameyoko Market Yokocho

    アメ横

    Vibrant open-air market streets with outdoor tables under train bridges. Great for fresh seafood skewers, Taiwanese dumplings, and market energy for teenagers.

    Authentic Local Food Rituals & Dining Hacks

    🍱 19:00 PM "Depachika Waribiki" Discount Run Basement food halls of luxury department stores (Isetan Shinjuku, Daimaru Tokyo Station) sticker unsold fresh Wagyu bentos, eel, and sushi with 20% to 50% off (割引) between 18:30 – 19:15 PM!
    🥢 Machichuka (町中華) Neighborhood Diners Everyday diners operated by elderly chefs for 40+ years. Complete set meals of Chahan (fried rice), Gyoza, and Shoyu Ramen cost only ~¥800 – ¥1,100 JPY per person.
    🍲 Yoshida Udon in Mt. Fuji (吉田のうどん) Local Fuji comfort food! Extraordinarily thick handmade wheat noodles in dashi broth topped with cabbage and braised meat seasoned with Suritane chili paste (~¥500 – ¥700 JPY).
    🍢 Torikizoku (鳥貴族) Izakaya Chain Japan's famous yakitori chain. Every item (skewers, fried chicken, drinks) is flat-rate ¥370 JPY. Ordered via multi-lingual touchscreen tablets!

    ⛩️ Comprehensive Master Local Hacks (100% Retained)

    1. Wise Card, Cash & Physical IC Card Logistics

    Wise Card + Seven Bank ATM Routine: Use Wise for direct debit payments at hotels, stores, and taxis. For cash, insert Wise card into Seven Bank ATMs (inside 7-Eleven). CRITICAL: Always select to be charged in Japanese Yen (JPY) on the ATM screen!

    Physical IC Cards (Welcome Suica / PASMO PASSPORT): Contactless cards cannot be tapped directly at train turnstiles. Buy tourist physical IC cards at Narita counters upon arrival.

    The Cash-Only Top-Up Rule: Train station machines ONLY accept Japanese Yen cash banknotes to top up physical IC cards. They do not accept credit or debit cards!

    2. Mandatory Booking Calendar Key Dates

    • July – Sept 2026: Book Tokyo base hotel (Richmond Asakusa) & Midweek Fuji Hotel (Tominoko).
    • October 19, 2026: Reserve teamLab Planets TOYOSU online (opens 60 days prior).
    • November 14, 2026 @ 10:00 AM JST: CRITICAL MILESTONE. Reserve JR Fuji Excursion train seats on JR East Reservation website (opens 30 days prior). Secure 6 seats on Train #7 immediately.
    • November 15, 2026: Reserve Shibuya Sky sunset timeslot (opens 4 weeks prior).

    3. Places & Things Only Locals Do

    Neighborhood Shotengai: Stroll Togoshi-Ginza Shotengai (1.3km longest street arcade, freshly fried croquettes) or Kichijoji Nakamichi-dori (voted #1 local neighborhood).

    The 07:30 AM Morning Shrine Rule: Visit Senso-ji or Meiji Jingu at 07:30 AM before tour buses arrive for quiet morning walks and resident monk sweeps.

    Sento Bathhouse Experience: Try Kosugiyu in Koenji or Jakotsu-yu near Asakusa (entry ~¥520 JPY per person).

    4. Fuji Driving & Transit Complexities

    Rental Gas Etiquette: At self-service stations, pump Regular gas (Color code RED). Say "Regular, Mantan" at full-service stations. Always request an ETC Toll Card at the rental counter.

    Shinjuku Station Platform 9/10: Follow JR Line signs for Platforms 9 & 10 (Limited Express platforms) for the Fuji Excursion.

    Shibuya Station 3rd-Floor Ginza Line: Unlike most subways, the Ginza Line platform is on the 3rd floor ABOVE ground inside Scramble Square.

    Weather Hack: Check Fujigoko Live Webcams (fujigoko.info/live/) on your phone before driving to confirm Mt. Fuji peak visibility.
